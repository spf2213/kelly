Kelly Engineering builds custom AI infrastructure for the essential parts of the American industrial economy: the brokers, distributors, manufacturers, and operators that keep the country running.

These businesses still run on paperwork and inboxes. We embed in their operations and build AI agents that read the documents, check the data, and draft the work. Your people approve it.

We charge 25% of what it saves, and nothing if it saves nothing. No slide decks. No hidden fees.

Our team has shipped mission-critical infrastructure at Palantir and General Dynamics, put satellites into orbit, run robotics at frontier labs, traded at top quant funds, and pushed ML research at MIT.

We’re named after Kelly Johnson, who built the SR-71 Blackbird at Lockheed’s Skunk Works with a small team in a rented circus tent. His motto: be quick, be quiet, be on time.
